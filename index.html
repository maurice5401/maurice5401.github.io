Clipper

Short clips from long videos, uploaded to my own channels.

Clipper is a private tool built and used by Maurice Hübscher. It runs locally on his own computer. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Finds strong moments in long-form videos and cuts them into vertical short clips with subtitles and music.
  • Uploads the finished clips as YouTube Shorts to YouTube channels that Maurice owns and manages. It uses the YouTube Data API v3 for this.
  • Reads the channel name and the view counts of its own uploads, so it can show which clips perform well.

Google sign-in

To upload, the channel owner signs in with Google and allows Clipper to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel and video information (youtube.readonly). Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Details on how this data is handled are in the privacy policy.
